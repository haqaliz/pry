# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Pry to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of experience,
background, identity, or discipline.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being respectful and constructive, especially in code review and disagreement.
- Welcoming newcomers and questions; assuming good intent.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people who use it.
- Being precise about what a finding does and does not prove.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Personal or political attacks, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Encouraging scans of targets without ownership or written authorization.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing
the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through the contact on the repository
owner's GitHub profile, or through a private security advisory if you prefer. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate
this code, and may ban repeat or severe offenders.
